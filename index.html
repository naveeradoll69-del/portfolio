01 / About

A little about me

What I care about when I build things.

I'm Syeda Naveera, an aspiring web developer who likes turning plain ideas into interfaces people enjoy using. My work sits between design and code — I care as much about how a page feels to scroll through as I do about how clean the markup is underneath.

Most of my time goes into HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and PHP, with Bootstrap 5 for fast, reliable structure. I build things to be responsive from the first line of CSS, not patched afterward.

Currently pursuing F.Sc (Pre-Medical) alongside an AI & Web Development program at Aptech, and building/refining CellInfo, a mobile-comparison web app — see it below. I'm looking for an internship or entry-level opportunity to grow these skills on real projects.
